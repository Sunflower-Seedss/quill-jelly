FIG. 010   /   QUILL / JOY IN JELLY
Inside the experiment

A small volume of tetrahedra, solved on the CPU and skinned on the GPU, held together by XPBD elastic and volume constraints. The hair and hoodie are a little firmer; the peach jelly gives. Fixed 120 Hz steps, strain limits and an inversion barrier keep it intact. Gravity, contact friction and internal damping let it settle.

Native WebGPU and WGSL. A back-face thickness pass drives light absorption, refracted studio light, Fresnel reflections and edge transmission. His sculpted star pupils, smile, freckles and tiny bubbles follow the same tetrahedra as the jelly. Soft filtered shadows ground the slice.

Drag on the slice to pull; Shift-drag or a second finger adds twist. Arrow keys nudge, Space pauses, R resets. Reduced-motion mode starts paused. Optical effects and physical units are artistic approximations.
